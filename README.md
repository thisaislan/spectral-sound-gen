Have you ever thought about listening to your code? Now you can!

</h4>

<br>
<br>

Spectral Sound Gen is a simple project created to allow you to hear your code, to do this you just need donwload the project and rund the follow command:

``python3 spectral_sound_gen.py <PATH_OF_YOUR_PROJECT_FOLDER>``

A file called ``compressed_data.wav`` will be created with the voice of your project, so listen with care :relieved:

<br>
<br>

In case you want to know what Spectral Sound Gen sound like, you can find this [here](https://github.com/thisaislan/spectral-sound-gen/blob/main/compressed_data.wav)
